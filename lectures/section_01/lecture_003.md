# Lecture 3: How This Course is Structured

## Curriculum Hierarchy
The course follows a progressive 8-tier curriculum:
1. **Foundations**: Python, Logging, Pydantic, NumPy/Pandas, Streamlit
2. **NLP & Transformers**: Mathematical & architectural underpinnings
3. **Generative AI Core**: Models, prompt strategies, Hugging Face
4. **LangChain & Vector Stores**: Embeddings, FAISS, ChromaDB
5. **RAG Mechanics**: Advanced chunking, HyDE, RRF, Ragas evaluation
6. **Agentic Workflows**: LangGraph states, decision loops, multi-agent networks
7. **GraphRAG & MCP**: Neo4j, Fine-Tuning, Model Context Protocol
8. **Cloud & Capstones**: AWS Bedrock, SageMaker, Docker, CrewAI
